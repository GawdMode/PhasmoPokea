# PhasmoPokéa 1.0

Become a Spirit Warden in Pokémon Crystal. Meet Ezra in Lavender's Soul House, accept nighttime investigations, gather evidence, identify the haunting Pokémon, locate its anchor, and cleanse it with the Spirit Seal.

## Requirements

- Gen1Recomp 0.2.59
- Pokémon Crystal Version (USA, Europe), Rev 1

## Investigations

- Ezra receives new cases after dark and contacts you through the Pokégear.
- Choose two Warden Tools before a case. Ghost Gurus may bring three.
- Use the Spirit Band and environmental evidence to gather anchor clues.
- Inspect office and broadcast desks from a first-person view.
- Search for the correct anchor object and apply the Spirit Seal.
- Wrong seals can enrage the spirit and begin a real-time chase.
- Report the haunting species to Ezra after returning.

## Warden ranks

- **Warden:** Candle, Incense, Writing Book
- **Spector Sage (15 points):** Binding Ash, Warding Charm
- **Ghost Guru (30 points):** Powerlight, Thermometer, and a third tool slot
- **Ace Exorcist (50 points):** Silph Sensor, UV Light

A successful cleansing awards 3 rank points. Correctly identifying the haunting Pokémon awards 1 additional point.

## Notes

The private five-floor haunted Radio Tower is separate from Goldenrod's normal Radio Tower maps. Ordinary Goldenrod story content is left unchanged.
