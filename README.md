# PhasmoPokéa

**A Pokémon Crystal ghost-investigation mod for Gen1Recomp.**

Become a Spirit Warden and investigate paranormal disturbances across the Pokémon world. Track an invisible spirit, gather clues, identify the haunting Pokémon, locate the object binding it to the area, and perform a successful cleansing before the investigation turns deadly.

Inspired by ghost-hunting games such as *Phasmophobia*, PhasmoPokéa transforms Pokémon Crystal into a deduction-focused paranormal investigation experience.

## Requirements

* **Gen1Recomp 0.2.59 or higher**
* **Pokémon Crystal Version (USA, Europe), Rev 1**

The original Pokémon Crystal ROM is not included.

## Getting Started

Travel to Lavender Town and enter the Soul House. Speak with **Ezra**, the resident Spirit Warden, to enroll.

Ezra will add himself to your Pokégear contacts. After enrollment, a new case may become available each night.

* Nighttime lasts from **6:00 PM through 3:59 AM**.
* The nightly case roll occurs on the first eligible overworld step after night begins.
* Cases cannot be accepted during the day.
* Return to Ezra when he calls to receive your assignment and select your equipment.

## Investigation Loop

Each official investigation tasks you with cleansing a private, haunted five-floor Radio Tower.

During a case, you must:

1. Select your Warden Tools.
2. Explore the darkened building.
3. Track the invisible roaming spirit.
4. Investigate furniture and environmental disturbances.
5. Use the Spirit Band to gather three anchor clues.
6. Interpret those clues to locate the spirit’s anchor object.
7. Apply the Spirit Seal to the correct object.
8. Escape if the spirit becomes enraged.
9. Report the haunting Pokémon’s species to Ezra.

Correctly sealing the anchor releases the spirit. Sealing the wrong object begins a dangerous real-time chase.

## Haunted Radio Tower

The investigation takes place in a private five-floor version of the Radio Tower featuring:

* Darkness and restricted visibility
* Desaturated paranormal lighting
* An invisible roaming spirit
* Activity-dependent manifestations
* Environmental disturbances
* Species-specific behavior
* First-person desk inspection scenes
* Atmospheric lore and contextual clues
* Hidden items and bonus evidence
* Unsettling phone calls
* Wrong-seal chase sequences
* TERRIFAINT consequences

The normal Goldenrod Radio Tower and its original story content remain unaffected.

## Evidence and Deduction

The Spirit Band can provide three clues describing the anchor’s:

* Floor or relative location
* Object type or physical character
* Position in relation to nearby scenery

Environmental searches may uncover additional follow-up hints. These can clarify vague evidence, but investigating too aggressively increases spirit activity.

The anchor must still be identified through deduction. Guessing recklessly can make the situation considerably worse.

## First-Person Inspection

Certain office desks and broadcast tables can be examined in a dedicated first-person scene.

Move the flashlight with the D-pad and inspect visible hotspots such as:

* Telephones
* Papers
* Drawers
* Shelves
* Potted plants
* Windows and glass panes
* Microphones
* Broadcast equipment
* Chairs

These scenes may contain atmospheric details, station lore, items, species hints, or anchor evidence. Lingering too long can draw the spirit closer.

## Warden Tools

The Spirit Band and Spirit Seal are standard equipment. Additional tools are selected before each case.

Most Wardens may bring **two tools**. Ghost Gurus and Ace Exorcists may bring **three**.

### Warden

* **Candle:** Expands visibility but can be extinguished.
* **Incense:** Reduces spirit activity once per case.
* **Writing Book:** May receive a bonus clue after the spirit reaches it.

### Spector Sage — 15 Rank Points

* **Binding Ash:** Slows the spirit and records a footprint when triggered.
* **Warding Charm:** Prevents one TERRIFAINT attack.

### Ghost Guru — 30 Rank Points

* **Powerlight:** Illuminates 40% more area than the Candle.
* **Thermometer:** Detects sudden temperature changes and nearby cold spots.
* Unlocks a **third Warden Tool slot**.

### Ace Exorcist — 50 Rank Points

* **Silph Sensor:** Tracks the spirit before three clues and detects anchor resonance afterward.
* **UV Light:** Temporarily reveals the entire room in green light and exposes the spirit’s silhouette when present.

## Warden Progression

Official cases award permanent Rank Points:

* **Successful cleansing:** 3 Rank Points
* **Correct spirit identification:** 1 Rank Point

A perfect investigation awards **4 Rank Points**.

Rank Points are permanent career progression and are not spent as currency.

| Rank         | Points | Unlocks                                  |
| ------------ | -----: | ---------------------------------------- |
| Warden       |      0 | Candle, Incense, Writing Book            |
| Spector Sage |     15 | Binding Ash, Warding Charm               |
| Ghost Guru   |     30 | Powerlight, Thermometer, third tool slot |
| Ace Exorcist |     50 | Silph Sensor, UV Light                   |

Ezra announces each promotion and explains the newly unlocked equipment.

## Spirit Species

Different spirits respond to activity in different ways. Their manifestations and aggression may provide subtle clues to their identity.

Correct identification is optional for completing a cleansing, but it awards an additional Rank Point.

## Installation

1. Install **Gen1Recomp 0.2.59**.
2. Configure it with a compatible Pokémon Crystal Rev 1 ROM.
3. Import or place the PhasmoPokéa ZIP in your Gen1Recomp mods directory.
4. Enable the mod and launch Pokémon Crystal.
5. Visit Lavender Town’s Soul House to begin.

Bug reports and gameplay feedback are welcome, especially regarding investigation balance, clue clarity, equipment behavior, and spirit activity.
